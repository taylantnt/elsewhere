# Game Structure & Level Flow

## Act 1: The Arrival (The Monologue)
- **Scene 1: The Airplane**: Introduction to the protagonist's internal monologue. Audio: `dream sequence 1`.
- **Scene 2: Narita/Haneda Airport**: The first hurdle. No phone, language barrier.
- **Scene 3: The Journey Home**: Multiple solutions (Taxi, Bus, Train). 
    - *Taxi*: High cost, low effort.
    - *Bus*: Medium cost, high time.
    - *Train*: Low cost, high difficulty (navigation puzzle).

## Act 2: Settling In (Daily Life)
- **Home Base**: Interacting with the Host Sister. First signs of the "glitch."
- **Locations**:
    - **Ramen Shop**: First meeting with the Ramen Guy.
    - **Bar**: Meeting the Wolf Master.
    - **Photography Club**: Meeting Sim.
    - **Akihabara / Harajuku**: Exploration and shopping mini-games.

## Act 3: The Glitch (The Mystery Deepens)
- **Dream Sequences**: The Robed Figure becomes more insistent.
- **Hospital Flashes**: The environment starts to show "seams" (digital artifacts, recurring NPC faces).
- **The Sister's Reveal**: Depending on friendship level, she starts dropping hints about the simulation.

## Act 4: The Revelation (The Climax)
- The player discovers the facility.
- Decision point: Escape, Stay, or Failure.

## Act 5: The Aftermath (Endings)
- **Ending A**: Waking up in the future Japan facility.
- **Ending B**: Continuing the simulation, blissfully unaware.
- **Ending C**: Assimilation failure - the cycle repeats with a "new subject."
