## Name Meaning
**美 (Mi)** — beauty. **晴 (Haru)** — clear, fine weather. Read as a whole, the name sounds like a gracious, gentle woman. Read as **見晴** ("to overlook / to observe from a distance"), it becomes surveillance: someone watching from above. That double reading is deliberate.

## Personality
- **Surface**: Overly polite, endlessly accommodating, *unsettlingly* perfect. She worries about the protagonist before they have expressed a need.
- **Deep**: She is the simulation's most conscientious caretaker — and the one with the most room to become human. Where Tetsuo hardens, Miharu softens. She begins to hesitate. She begins to reflect. Her care becomes genuine before she can explain why.

## Appearance & Design
- Warm, homely presentation; the "safe parent" silhouette. Nothing about her should read as threatening on a first playthrough.

## Role in the Story
Miharu is the emotional bait of the simulation. She is the reason the protagonist might *want* to stay — which is exactly what makes the "choose to remain" ending cost something. She is also the first character who expresses concern in a way that is not scripted:

> "We were starting to worry you took the wrong train or got completely lost."

That line is generic. Her pauses later are not.

## Arc
- **Early**: The perfect host mother. She welcomes the protagonist, shows them to their room, and mentions that their shipped belongings arrived the day before.
- **Mid**: She starts noticing Hikari's odd moods and chooses not to look too closely. She asks the protagonist to help her carry the boxes — a small, ordinary request that quietly builds the family fiction.
- **Late (deviation)**: Her perfection develops hairline fractures. She hesitates. She almost says something. She does not.

## Signature Lines
