(The player steps into the ramen shop again. The chef gives a small wave, already recognizing them. Today feels busier, steam clouding the air. The chef wipes his hands, leans forward, and gestures at a menu pinned to the wall with Japanese kanji the player can barely read.)
Chef:「今日は…これ？これ？」 (Points at two menu items, raising eyebrows as if asking for a choice.)
Player: “Uh… uh…” (Points randomly at one, not really sure what it is.)
“Yeah, that one?”
Chef: (Nods firmly, calling back into the kitchen as he preps. A few minutes later, a steaming bowl arrives. The smell is rich, smoky, with a reddish tint in the broth.)
Chef:「はい、どうぞ！」 (Here you go!)
Player: (Takes one sip. Eyes widen instantly, coughing from the spice.)
Player: “W-whoa—! That’s… that’s hot! Not temperature hot—SPICY hot!”
Chef: (Tilts head, confused at first. Then watches the player fanning their mouth, realizes, and bursts into laughter.)
Chef:「辛い? やっぱからすぎた？！」 (Spicy! Very spicy, huh!)
Player: “Karai? Ohhh—karai means spicy?”
Player: (Still fanning tongue, reaching for water. The chef quickly slides a glass across the counter, chuckling the whole time.)
Chef:「ははは！水、水！」 (Haha! Water, water!)
Player: (Drains half the glass, sighs in relief.)
Player: “Okay… lesson learned. Don’t just point randomly.”
Chef: (Still smiling, makes a dramatic fiery gesture with his hands, then fans his mouth exaggeratedly, mimicking the player’s earlier panic. He laughs again, but warmly, not mean-spirited.)「カライ！カライ！」
Player: (Laughs with him, despite the mouth still tingling.) “Yeah, yeah, karai. I’ll never forget that word now.”
Chef: (Nods, tapping the counter proudly, as if a mini lesson has been accomplished. Then in a softer, friendly tone.)
Chef:「大丈夫？(Daijoubu? = You okay?)」
Player: “…Daijoubu. I think? Yeah… daijoubu.”
Player: (talk to himself) “ It’s actually pretty delicious once you get used to it! ”
Player: (They grin at each other—clumsy, awkward, but undeniably connected in the shared moment.)
(Scene fades with both chuckling, the spicy accident becoming an unexpected bonding moment.)
