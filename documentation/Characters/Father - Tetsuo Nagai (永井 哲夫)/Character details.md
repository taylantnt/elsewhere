# Character Profile: Tetsuo Nagai (永井 哲夫)

## Snapshot

| Field | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Role** | Host father |
| **Location** | The host family home |
| **Archetype** | The Schedule / The System's Voice |
| **Affection Variable** | *(none — he is not a confidant)* |
| **Language** | Japanese; formal and slightly archaic |
| **Confidant Theme** | Order, propriety, and whether a man made of routine can change |
| **Leaves / Closes?** | No — but his *state* changes with the protagonist's choices. |

## Name Meaning
**哲 (Tetsu)** — wisdom. **夫 (Fu)** — man. "Wise husband." The name sounds admirable, which is the trick: he is the most rigid person in the house.

## Personality
- **Surface**: Traditional, precise, obsessed with schedule and neatness. Everything has a proper way.
- **Deep**: He genuinely wants to treat the protagonist like family — he says so on the first night. But his affection can only express itself as rules. He is a man who believes that if everyone keeps to the routine, nothing bad can happen.

## Appearance & Design
- Formal, buttoned-up, orderly. Nothing out of place.
- **He still uses a flip phone from ten years ago** — a small but load-bearing detail. He distrusts "gadget nonsense" and prefers letters and face-to-face conversation.

## Role in the Story
Tetsuo is one of the most useful pieces of foreshadowing in the game, because he is the character who is *most obviously scripted*. His dialogue is correct, his hospitality is technically flawless, and he never deviates. When the protagonist starts noticing that the world is too tidy, Tetsuo is the evidence.

## Arc
Two possible outcomes, chosen by how much the protagonist deviates from the expected path:

| Outcome | Condition | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Never breaks character** | The protagonist follows the intended route | He remains the perfect host father to the very end, including in the epilogue. Reads on a second playthrough as a system asset. |
| **Corrupted by deviation** | The protagonist repeatedly breaks routine / pursues the truth | Small cracks appear: a repeated sentence, a pause that is a beat too long, an answer to a question nobody asked. He is the first *non-Hikari* character to visibly glitch. |

## Signature Lines
- "And I'm Tetsuo Nagai. So, you finally found our home."
- "We keep things the proper way here — none of that new gadget nonsense. A good letter or a face-to-face talk works best."
- "This is our first time hosting someone from abroad. We'll treat you like family here."
