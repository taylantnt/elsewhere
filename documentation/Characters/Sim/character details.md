# Character Profile: Sim (Photo Circle Host)

## Snapshot

| Field | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Role** | Founder and host of "Circle," a free photography meetup group |
| **Location** | A modest studio / café back room where the group meets |
| **Archetype** | The Creative Community Builder / The Lonely Mentor |
| **Affection Variable** | `sim_affection` |
| **Language** | Switches fluidly between Japanese and English to include everyone |
| **Confidant Theme** | Connection, being seen, and creative courage |
| **Leaves / Closes?** | **Yes — he moves to another city, and the meetups end.** |

## Personality
- **Surface**: Warm, encouraging, patient. Genuinely charismatic when mentoring a room full of people.
- **Deep**: Awkward in one-to-one introductions and quietly insecure. Years of magazine rejections taught him that his work was never quite enough. The club is his proof that he is not alone.

## Appearance & Design
- Camera always within reach, functional but creative clothing.
- The camera strap is his signature prop — and the object he eventually gives away.

## Backstory
Before founding Circle, Sim had no friends and no network: only submissions that went unanswered. He built the group as a free creative space — portfolio practice, no gatekeeping, and a reason to be around people. Photography is how he anchors himself to a city that had been indifferent to him.

## Confidant Arc (Interactions 1–7)
| # | Beat |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Awkward introductions. He explains the group's purpose: free space, portfolios, friendships. "Next time, you shoot." |
| 2 | He remembers the protagonist's name and teaches camera basics in both languages. |
| 3 | First success — the protagonist's photo comes out genuinely good. Sim admits his own years of rejection. |
| 4 | The group grows. He manages the crowd warmly and hints at wanting an exhibition one day. |
| 5 | A quiet night. He admits the club exists because he had nobody, and thanks the protagonist: "This group feels real because you're here too." |
| 6 | A magazine editor has contacted him about his work — real recognition, but it means relocating. He promises an exhibition first. |
| 7 | **Farewell at the exhibition.** He announces he's leaving for the job and gives the protagonist a camera strap identical to his own. |

## How His Arc Ends (The Departure Theme)
Sim's growth is real: he gets the recognition he spent years chasing. The cost is that the thing he built — the club, the studio, the weekly ritual — dissolves the moment he succeeds, and the protagonist is left holding a camera strap instead of a friend.

**In-game consequence**: after Interaction 7 the Photo Studio / Circle meetups are gone. The location either closes or is populated only by unfamiliar faces, and the **Photo Mode** mentoring sessions end. `sim_affection` is locked.

## What He Symbolises in the Simulation
Sim is the "escape fantasy" mirror. He proves he can leave — for a better city, a better life — while the protagonist cannot leave the world they are in. When Hikari later starts guiding the protagonist toward reality, Sim's departure is the emotional rehearsal for it.
