# Character Profile: Sim (Host of Circle)

## Snapshot

| Field | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Role** | Host and organiser of the university's **Photography Club — Circle** — an outsider who comes in to guide it |
| **Location** | The university's club room, second floor, the door with the tape on it. **He is not a student there** |
| **Archetype** | The Creative Community Builder / The Lonely Mentor |
| **Affection Variable** | `sim_affection` |
| **Language** | Switches fluidly between Japanese and English to include everyone |
| **Confidant Theme** | Connection, being seen, and creative courage |
| **Leaves / Closes?** | **He moves to another city; Circle goes on hold until a new student host is found.** |

## Personality
- **Surface**: Warm, encouraging, patient. Genuinely charismatic when mentoring a room full of people.
- **Deep**: Awkward in one-to-one introductions and quietly insecure. Years of magazine rejections taught him that his work was never quite enough. The club is his proof that he is not alone.

## Appearance & Design
- Camera always within reach, functional but creative clothing.
- The camera strap is his signature prop — and the object he eventually gives away.

## Backstory
Before Circle became a community, Sim had no friends and no network: only submissions that went unanswered. He built the group as a free creative space — portfolio practice, no gatekeeping, and a reason to be around people. Photography is how he anchors himself to a city that had been indifferent to him.

## Confidant Arc (Interactions 1–8)
| # | Beat |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Awkward introductions. He explains the group's purpose: free space, portfolios, friendships, and has the newcomer photograph the room before anyone talks. |
| 2 | He remembers the protagonist's name and teaches camera basics in both languages. |
| 3 | First success — the protagonist's photo comes out genuinely good. Sim admits his first proud photograph was rejected four times and ignored by a fifth. |
| 4 | The group grows. He manages the crowd warmly and hints at wanting an exhibition one day. |
| 5 | A quiet night. He admits the club exists because he had nobody, and says the protagonist is in a folder now. |
| 6 | A magazine editor has contacted him about his work — real recognition, but taking it could mean relocating. He promises an exhibition first. |
| 7 | **Farewell at the exhibition.** He announces his magazine commission and gives the protagonist the worn strap he has used for years. |
| 8 | **Epilogue: the club on hold.** The school has paused Circle until a new student host is found. Nami is on the campus steps with the exhibition in a cardboard box. The same steps, box and maybe replay on every later visit. |

## How His Arc Ends (The Departure Theme)
Sim's growth is real: he gets the recognition he spent years chasing. The cost is that the thing he built — the club, the room, the weekly ritual — goes on hold the moment he succeeds, and the protagonist is left holding his worn strap while Nami tapes up the exhibition prints.

**In-game consequence**: after Interaction 7 he stops coming in. The room is
still there, but the club is on hold: a club needs a faculty advisor and a
student host, and it has neither. The **Photo Mode** mentoring sessions end with
him, and `sim_affection` is locked. The eighth visit is the repeatable epilogue
on the campus steps, with Nami and the box of prints and the maybe of a new host.

## What He Symbolises in the Simulation
Design thesis: Sim is an "escape fantasy" mirror. He proves he can leave — for a better city, a better life — while the protagonist cannot leave the world they are in. The shipped scene complicates that reading: he talks about carrying the room with him, hands over the strap so the protagonist can keep seeing, and stays in touch over WIRE. When Hikari later starts guiding the protagonist toward reality, Sim's departure is the emotional rehearsal for it.
