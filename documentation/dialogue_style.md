# Dialogue Style Rule: No Em Dashes

## The rule

Never use an em dash in dialogue. This applies to every spoken line, choice
label, character memory, and WIRE or phone message in `game/`, and to the quoted
dialogue in the design documents under `documentation/Locations/`.

Use a period, a comma, a colon, or an ellipsis instead. A plain hyphen is only
for compound words such as "off-centre". Narration that sits inside a scene is
written the same way, so keep it free of em dashes too.

Prose that is only planning or commentary, such as `story_treatment.md`, may
still use a dash for an aside. Nothing a character says or types may use one.

## Why

The game's voice is short and unhurried. An ellipsis already does the pause,
and a period does the stop. An em dash reads as a typesetting flourish and
breaks the plain spoken rhythm the characters share.
