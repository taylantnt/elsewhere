[Late evening. A narrow shop with a dark blue noren, a bowl painted on it in white. Steam, broth, fat hitting a hot pan. The player lifts the cloth and goes in.]

[Four seats, a counter, a wall of steam. One man behind it in an apron and a towel tied around his head. He does not look up. The board is handwritten and unreadable; the prices underneath are numbers.]

Chef (to the room): 「いらっしゃい。」 (Irasshai — welcome.)
Player (thinking): I know that one. Every shop in this country says it.

[The player puts a thousand yen on the counter. The chef pushes it back with one finger and grunts at the empty stool: afterwards.]

Player choice — how to order:
- "Order in English." — he waves at the whole board. (Nerve)
- "Point at something." — the second line down; he glances at the finger and says nothing. (Insight)
- "Ask for his recommendation." — 「おすすめ」; he points at the second line and waits for a nod. (Grace)

Chef: 「あいよ。」 (Aiyo — sure.)
[He barks an order at nobody; he is the kitchen and the waiter.]

[A bowl arrives. Almost-black shoyu broth, more pork than noodle, a hill of green onions. Not what the player pictured.]

Player choice — the broth:
- "Ask what kind of broth it is." — he points at the dark bottle beside the stove; soy sauce in a pork broth. (Insight)
- "Try it before asking." — the spoon is already in hand; he says 「どうぞ。」 (Here you go.)

[The player eats. Rich, smoky, far too much; the second mouthful is better.]
Player (half under his breath): …This is actually amazing.
Chef (to the pot): 「うまい、だろ？」 (Umai, daro? — Tasty, right?)

Player choice — the word:
- "Ask what it means." — he repeats 「うまい。」 slowly, points at the bowl, and grunts yes. This is where the player learns *umai*. (Insight)
- "Thank him properly." — "Arigatou gozaimasu." (Grace)
- "Ask what gives it the smoky flavour." — he taps the grill with his ladle.
- "Point at the bottle and wait for him to name it." — he says its name slowly and watches to see if the player got it.

[He snorts once, killed immediately. The player slurps badly, gets broth on the counter, and is not scolded.]

[Two men come in and eat in focused silence. The player copies their grip on the chopsticks, spoon in the other hand. The bowl is finished to the pattern on the bottom.]

[The thousand yen goes back on the counter. He takes it, counts change without looking, and sets a hundred yen beside the player's hand: 900 for the bowl.]

[The chef has been eyeing the phone left beside the money.]
Chef: 「WIRE、やってるか」 (Are you on WIRE? — with the English word dropped in.)
[He turns a laminated card with a QR square and six English words in someone else's handwriting. 「読め」 (Read it / scan it). The player holds the phone over it and a name appears on the list.]
Player: So I know if you're shut before I walk down here for nothing.

[He turns the card back to himself and goes to the pots. A queued WIRE advert waits for the phone's data line: 「新メニュー。900円。水曜日は休み。」]

[Outside, the street is dark and the player knows exactly where he is: two convenience stores twenty metres away, the street he walked this morning without looking. Home is four minutes. It took far longer to find the shop.]

[Later that evening the walk-alone route carries this into the first dinner: he tells Hikari and his father he is still full from ramen.]
