[Late night. Narrow ramen shop. The air is thick with steam and broth. The chef, a middle-aged man in a simple apron and bandana, barely looks up as the player enters.]
Chef (gruff, without much eye contact): 「いらっしゃい…」 (Irasshai… welcome.)
Player (hesitant, in English): “Uh… hi. Ramen, please?”
Chef (tilts head, not understanding, then waves vaguely to the menu board—entirely in Japanese.)
Player (squints, points randomly at a line of text): “Uh… this one? Please?”
Chef: 「あいよ。」 (Aiyo, sure.)
(He barks the order to the kitchen—well, to himself, since he is the kitchen.)
[Minutes later, a steaming bowl is set down. It’s nothing like what the player expected—dark broth, heavy pork, extra garlic.]
Player (blinks, whispers to self): “This… definitely isn’t what I pointed at.”
Chef (grunts, crossing arms, as if to say “Eat.”): 「どうぞ。」 (Dōzo… here you go.)
[Player hesitates, then takes a bite. Flavor hits hard, rich and smoky. The chef glances over briefly, unreadable.]
Player (surprised, speaking half to self): “…This is actually amazing.”
Chef (catching the tone if not the words, mutters): 「うまい、だろ？」 (Umai, daro? — Tasty, right?)
Player (has never heard the word): “Sumimasen — what does that mean?”
Chef (says it again, slower, and points at the bowl with the ladle): 「うまい。」 (Umai.)
Player (trying it back): “Umai?”
Chef (grunts yes, turns back to the pots — that was the lesson): 「うん。うまい。」 (Un. Umai.)
[The chef snorts softly, a shadow of amusement flickering across his face before he turns back to the simmering pots. The player slurps clumsily, broth splashing a little. The chef doesn’t comment, but his eyes flicker with quiet judgment-turned-acceptance.]
[Scene fades with the sound of boiling broth and clumsy slurping.]
