[The shop is loud with the noise of a place that has become a destination. Customers speak languages the player does not recognize and eat with the same focused silence as everyone else. Word has traveled; the ramen is no longer a secret.]

[Chef moves faster than the player has ever seen him — pots, noodles, broth, garnish, every motion instinct. He catches the player's eye and points to the counter: 「特等席。」 (Premium seat.) He is offering the show.]

[The stool beside the player is empty for three seconds. A woman in a suit sits down; she looks as if she has not stopped moving in weeks.]

Woman: "I heard about this place from a coworker. She said the broth saves lives."
Player: "She wasn't wrong."
Woman: "I need saved. I haven't eaten a real meal since Tuesday."

Player choice:
- "Introduce her to Chef." — 「ようこそ。」 (Welcome) and 「豚骨、いいよ。」 (Tonkotsu is good). The chef's rule for the students bends for a first-timer. (Grace)
- "Let her discover it herself." — she watches him work, eyes wide; the player remembers his own first time. (Insight)
- "Tell her what to order." — "Get the tonkotsu. Trust me." It takes twelve hours; it takes thirty years. That is why it matters. (Nerve)

[The rush continues for an hour. Chef does not stop, complain, or seem tired. When the line dissolves he leans on the counter and exhales. For the first time the player notices the gray in his hair.]

Chef: 「疲れた。」 (Tired.)
Player: "You should rest."
Chef: 「後で。」 (Later.) There is always more to do.

Player choice:
- "Can I help clean?" — 「客は、食べるだけ。」 (Customers only eat) and then 「でも、ありがとう。」 (But thank you). The refusal is kind. (Grace)
- "You work too hard." — 「好きだから、楽しい。」 (I like it, so it's fun), then a hand on the chest and two fingers: two days. (Insight)
- "Do you ever think about retiring?" — 「ない。止まったら、死ぬ。」 (No. If I stop, I die.) (Insight)

[Chef brings a bowl. It is the regular bowl, but the broth is richer and the noodles springier: the bowl of a man who found his rhythm and rode it.]

Chef: 「今日、よかった。」 (Today was good.)
Player: "The shop was full."
Chef: 「うん。」

[WIRE message: the woman in the suit came for the first time, sent by a coworker; he wonders whether she will come back. The shop grows whether he wills it or not.]
