[The shop is full. Every seat taken, a patient line at the door. Chef sees the player through the steam and points to a small table in the corner — a promotion from the counter.]

[The table wobbles. The group beside the player is speaking English; two students are not from here.]

Student 1: "I don't know what any of this says. Just point at something?"
Student 2: "That's what I did last time. It worked."
Student 1: "What if I point at the wrong thing?"
Student 2: "Then you learn something about yourself."

[Chef overhears without interrupting; his mouth curves at the corner.]

Player choice:
- "Tell them the second line, then let them taste why." — the player explains the house special in English. The students relax about pointing. (Grace)
- "Let them figure it out." — the arguing is part of the experience; Chef catches the player's eye and nods. (Insight)
- "Ask Chef to recommend." — 「男子、豚骨。女子、塩。」 (Men get tonkotsu, women get salt), delivered as gravity rather than preference. The player tells the students only: "Trust him." (Nerve)

[The orders go out. The students eat with the enthusiasm of people discovering something they did not know they needed. Chef watches them with the attention of a man measuring, not judging.]

[After the rush, Chef brings the player a bowl that is not the regular one: a soft-boiled egg in the centre, perfectly round, perfectly yellow.]

Chef: 「特別。」 (Special.)
Player: "For me?"
[Chef's line depends on what the player did:]
- If the player helped in English: 「英語、教えた。」 (You taught them English.)
- If Chef recommended: 「助けたかった。」 (I wanted to help.)
- If the player watched: 「学生、自分で決めた。」 (The students decided for themselves.)

Player choice — how to receive it:
- "I was just being helpful." — 「助けるのは、いいことだ。」 (Helping is a good thing.) (Grace)
- "I like watching people find good food." — 「秘密じゃない。でも、嬉しい。」 (It isn't a secret. But I'm glad.) (Insight)
- "Did you see them enjoying it?" — 「見てた。最初、分からない。後、分かる。」 (I saw. At first you don't understand; afterwards you do.) (Insight)

[The player eats the egg. The yolk coats the noodles. Chef asks 「どう？」 and the player answers with the one word he owns: "Umai." He nods once and goes back to the pots.]

[The WIRE message matches the branch: the player taught them the second line in English; Chef recommended tonkotsu; or the students ordered by pointing and laughing. In every version they ate well, and that was enough.]
