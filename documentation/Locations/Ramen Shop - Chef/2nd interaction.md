[The ramen shop. Quieter than last time: the same two men eat in focused silence at the other end of the counter. The chef is at the stove, conducting the pots with chopsticks.]

Chef: 「いらっしゃい。」 (Welcome.)
[He does not turn around. He knows the step. The player says good evening; he grunts, then points at the second line on the menu with his ladle.]

Chef: 「それ。」 (That.)
Player (thinking): That's the one I had last time.

[The stool has developed a memory of the player's posture, or the player has developed a memory of the stool.]

Chef: 「前、うまかった？」 (Delicious last time?)
Player (thinking): I recognize the sound from last time. Delicious? Did I like it?
Player: "Hai. Umai."

[He nods once, satisfied, and returns to the pots.]

Player choice:
- "Ask what he's making today." — 「豚骨」 (tonkotsu) and 「朝から」 (since morning). (Insight)
- "Watch him work silently." — 「見てる？」 / 「いい目だ。」 (Watching? / Good eyes.) (Grace)
- "Thank him for the last bowl." — he grunts 「男子。」 and adds a little more green onion to the bowl he is preparing. (Nerve)

[He sets the bowl down. Lighter broth than last time, no less rich, firmer noodles. He is teaching without teaching.]

Chef: 「熱い。」 (Hot.)
Player: "Arigatou."

[The player eats. Steam between them like a curtain that can be seen through. Chef watches the door more than he watches the player, but his attention is always in the room.]

Chef: 「毎日？」 (Every day?)
Player: "Iie. Not every day. But I like coming here."
Chef: 「いい。」 (Good.)

[He wipes the counter with a cloth that has seen years of broth and steam.]

Chef: 「水曜、休み。」 (Wednesday, closed.)
Player (thinking): The shop's card said so the first night. One day a week this man refuses to work, and he wants me to remember which one.
Chef: 「休むのも、仕事だ。」 (Resting is work too.)
Player (thinking): That is one I will have to work out myself.

[WIRE message, queued for the next day: 「スープは十二時間。残りは二日。どちらも一杯のうちに入っている。」 — the broth takes twelve hours; both the long boil and the wait go into one bowl.]
