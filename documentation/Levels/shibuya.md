## Scene

The scramble is a performance that happens every two minutes. The screens sell
something; the crowd goes somewhere; the two have nothing to do with each other.
When the light changes a thousand people walk into each other and nobody
touches.

The line: it is the most crowded place he has stood in, and the most alone.

Three choices:

- cross with the crowd (Nerve);
- watch the crossing from above (Insight);
- find the Hachikō statue and wait (Grace).
