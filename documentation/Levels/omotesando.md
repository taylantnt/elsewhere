## Scene

A street built to look like a forest and priced like a museum. Glass and
concrete shops, tall trees that make the buildings feel humble. Then the shops
end and the torii begins: the city gets quiet all at once. The same street, two
countries, and the border is a gate.

Three choices:

- purify at the temizuya (Grace);
- walk the gravel path and listen (Insight);
- write a wish on an ema (Nerve). He writes a question instead.

The line the evening repeats: the city makes you loud; the shrine makes you
listen.
