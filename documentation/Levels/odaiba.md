## Scene

Odaiba is an island built to be the future and then forced to live in it. The
streets are too wide, the buildings the right height and color, the water on
three sides like a moat that got out. A giant white robot statue stands over the
plaza with one hand raised.

Three choices:

- take the photo everyone takes (Insight);
- eat at the conveyor-belt sushi place (Nerve);
- sit on the fake beach and look at the city (Grace).
