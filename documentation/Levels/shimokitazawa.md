## Scene

What Harajuku would be if it stopped performing. Narrow streets, vintage shops,
a live house with a hand-painted sign and a door like a fridge. Nobody is
dressed for a photograph; everybody is dressed for themselves, which is a
different effort.

The line: the first place in the city that has felt like a neighborhood.

Three choices:

- buy a used record (Insight);
- go into a live house (Nerve);
- sit in a coffee shop and listen (Grace).
