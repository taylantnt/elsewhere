## Tour

The fifth Message Hikari outing, and the one that is most about *her*. The hook
is a record shop, not a landmark: "There is a record I almost bought once. I
want to see if it is still there." Beats:

1. The small gate from the station and the cat in the gap between the bicycle
   shop and the wall. She knows both. The street is narrow, hand-painted, and
   dressed for nobody — "everybody here is dressed as themselves."
2. The history, on the walk: after the war the rent was cheap, so 小劇場 — small
   fifty-seat theaters — moved in, then students, then record shops. It has
   never been rebuilt into anything newer, because nobody could agree what to
   rebuild it into.
3. The used record shop: 中古, the good things sink into the third crate, and
   the record she almost bought at nineteen is still there. She holds it, puts
   it back exactly where she found it. He recognizes a different song from a
   train commercial, and she is almost impressed — that is how music gets into
   a city.
4. The live house with a door like a fridge. Bass through the door, a setlist
   taped beside it, a band she knows that nobody knows. She almost goes in and
   does not: "if I go in now I will expect it to feel like it did, and it will
   not." Then the line that opens her up: she used to come here when she was
   "somebody who went to shows and did not have to be the one who knew where
   everything was."
5. The coffee shop, where the owner remembers her order after nine years. She
   says what she wanted to be and what she does not miss: not the music, but
   that she did not have to be good at it. He points out that he is that person
   right now. She brings up Sim's Circle on purpose — being seen and not being
   seen are both valid, and she is learning the difference.
6. The walk back. A hundred small rules about what you are allowed to do in
   each room — "that is the thing to learn."

Five menus cover her past, the theaters, the record, the live house, the coffee
shop, and the walk back. Each pays a micro reward once; finishing pays a final
Insight tick. `test_hikari_tour_shimokitazawa` in the console plays the full tour.

## Solo scene

He takes the small gate; the cat may or may not recognize him. Four choices:
buy the used record (Insight), go into a live house (Nerve), sit in the coffee
shop (Grace), or walk the little theaters without going in (Insight). Then he
returns to the door, the record shop, or the cat for a second, quieter beat.

The theme is the opposite of Harajuku: a place where nobody is dressed for a
photograph, where being ordinary is the whole welcome.
