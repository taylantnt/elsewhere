## Scene

April hanami: blue tarps, cardboard, salarymen with loosened ties, old men with
a bottle and a radio. The blossoms are already falling. The ground is pink where
people have not walked.

The line the evening repeats: the flowers are an excuse; the sitting is the
point.

Three choices:

- sit under the blossoms and watch (Insight);
- accept the cup from the old men (Nerve);
- walk to the museum and not go in (Grace).
