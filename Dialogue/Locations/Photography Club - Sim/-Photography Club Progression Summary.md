Progression Summary
    •    1: First meetup, awkward introductions in the studio; Sim explains the group’s purpose—free creative space, portfolio building, making friends.
    •    2: Recognition, Sim remembers your name and shows you how to handle the camera basics; switches smoothly between Japanese/English for the group.
    •    3: Small success—your photo comes out surprisingly well; Sim encourages you, reveals his struggles to get published despite years of trying.
    •    4: More participants join; Sim manages them warmly, shares stories of past failures, hinting at his quiet ambition to “one day make it.”
    •    5: Sim reveals his loneliness before creating the group—no friends, no network; but now he’s surrounded by people, thanks to photography.
    •    6: Breakthrough—he casually mentions being contacted by a magazine editor about one of his photos; the first real sign of recognition.
    •    7: Farewell—Sim announces he’s moving to another city for a professional photography job; emotional goodbye, gratitude to the group that gave him courage.

    Encounter 1 — First Meetup
    •    Player steps into the modest studio; Sim greets them warmly but introductions feel a little awkward.
    •    Sim explains the group’s purpose: a free, creative space for portfolios, practice, and friendship.
    •    Group feels welcoming yet slightly intimidating; player is mostly observing.
    •    Ending: Sim encourages the player to return — “next time, you shoot.”
    •    Tone: tentative, slightly shy, but with seeds of belonging.

Encounter 2 — Recognition
    •    Sim remembers the player’s name, making the space feel more personal.
    •    He patiently shows camera basics, switching between Japanese and English to include everyone.
    •    Player clumsily follows instructions; the group chuckles in good humor.
    •    Ending: Sim pats the player’s shoulder, promises they’ll improve faster than they expect.
    •    Tone: warm, encouraging — the walls start to come down.

Encounter 3 — First Success
    •    The studio buzzes with energy; Sim pushes the player to shoot their first real photo.
    •    After some shaky attempts, the player captures a crisp, surprisingly good shot.
    •    Group applauds genuinely; Rika (a member) cheers them on.
    •    Sim opens up about his own struggles: years of rejection from magazines.
    •    Ending: Sim insists the player treasure this “first good shot” as the true beginning.
    •    Tone: milestone warmth; pride mixed with Sim’s quiet vulnerability.
    
Encounter 4 — Growing Community
    •    More participants join; the group feels lively, with chatter and playful energy.
    •    Sim balances mentoring with managing the crowd, showing his charisma.
    •    Other members start talking directly to the player, welcoming them as “one of us.”
    •    Hints of a bigger dream appear: Sim mentions someday wanting their best works shown together.
    •    Ending: group takes a spontaneous photo together — laughter and flashes sealing the moment.
    •    Tone: social, communal, belonging expands beyond Sim.

Encounter 5 — Sim’s Loneliness Revealed
    •    A quieter night in the studio; fewer people around.
    •    Sim admits that before starting the club, he had no friends, no network — only rejection.
    •    He gestures to the group photos and says they’re proof he’s no longer alone.
    •    The bond with the player deepens; silences are now comfortable.
    •    Ending: Sim thanks the player specifically — “this group feels real because you’re here too.”
    •    Tone: intimate, personal, bittersweet honesty.

Encounter 6 — Breakthrough & Foreshadowing
    •    The studio feels celebratory; members bring their best photos to share.
    •    Sim reveals he was contacted by a magazine editor about his work.
    •    This isn’t just recognition — it’s an opportunity, but it means relocating to work closely with them.
    •    Sim reassures the group: before he leaves, there will be an exhibition showcasing everyone’s best shots.
    •    Ending: the group buzzes with excitement — their first exhibition is set, though Sim looks quietly conflicted.
    •    Tone: hopeful, charged with ambition but underscored by looming change.

Encounter 7 — The Exhibition & Farewell
    •    A gallery exhibition: members’ photos line the walls; the club’s energy fills the space.
    •    Visitors admire their work; laughter and pride mingle with nerves.
    •    In the middle of it all, Sim announces he’s leaving for his photography career.
    •    He gives the player a keepsake camera strap — identical to his own — symbolizing their bond.
    •    Final group photo taken together, sealing the memory of the club.
    •    Ending: Sim promises, “Someday, we’ll walk with our cameras again.” Fade on bittersweet closure.
    •    Tone: celebratory yet gut-wrenching; gratitude and growth, ending on hope.
