The ramen shop. The player steps inside again. The air is thick with broth and steam. The chef looks up, surprised but pleased.
Chef: 「おっ…また来たね。」(Oh… you came again.)
(He gives a small nod of recognition.)
Player: “Uh—hi. Yeah. Uh… your ramen was really good last time.”
(The chef squints, not fully catching it, but senses the enthusiasm.)
Chef: 「ラーメン…好き？」 (Ramen… you like?)
(He leans forward, hand cupped to his ear, playful testing tone.)
Player: “Yes! I like. Very good.”
(The player nods emphatically. The chef chuckles.)
Chef: 「おいしい。」(Oishii.)
(He points at the bowl of ramen steaming in front of another customer, then back to the player. He says it slowly, clearly, as if teaching a child.)
Player: “O… oishii?”
(It comes out clumsy, awkward.)
Chef: 「そうそう！おいしい！」(Yes, yes! Delicious!)
(He laughs warmly, clapping his hands once. The sound bounces in the small shop. A couple of regulars smile faintly at the exchange.)
Player: “Oishii.”
(Repeats with a bit more confidence, smiling now.)
Chef: 「いいね〜！」 (Good, good!)
(He gives a little thumbs-up. Then, teasingly slow, he repeats: 「お〜い〜し〜い〜」*)
Player: “O… i… shiii.”
(The player exaggerates the sounds, trying to mimic him. It comes out funny. The chef bursts into a small laugh but nods, clearly approving the effort.)
Chef: 「上手だね。」(You did well.)
(He wipes his hands on a towel, then gestures at an empty seat, inviting the player to sit.)
(The atmosphere feels different this time—lighter. Less like a stranger stepping into an unknown place, more like being welcomed into something alive and human. The chef keeps sneaking glances at the player while working, as though quietly amused that this foreigner came back a second time.)
Player (to self): “…Yeah. Feels a little less… foreign now.”
(Scene fades)
