[Late evening drizzle. The noren curtain sways; the bell gives its familiar chirin. The shop smells like soy, steam, and grilled pork. Only two other stools are taken. The Chef glances up, eyes crinkling. No words yet—just a small nod.]
Chef: 「…おかえり。」
[He taps the counter twice, sets down a water glass. Before you can gesture, he’s already moving: stock, ladle, the soft clack of bowls.]
Player (internal): He didn’t ask. He just… knows?
[The Chef lifts a bowl, tastes the broth, adjusts with a neat splash of tare. He plates an ajitama half, a careful fold of nori, and fewer drops of oil than usual.]
Chef (setting the bowl): 「しょうゆ、あぶら すくなめ。…ね。」
[Shoyu, less oil. Right.]
[He slides a tiny saucer across, an extra half-egg.]
Chef (almost looking embarrassed): 「サービズ…じゃない、サービス。」
[He corrects himself with a tiny huff. “Service.” A freebie.]
Player: “Arigatō… gozaimasu.”
[You try to mirror his cadence. He gives a short, satisfied grunt.]
[You slurp. The broth is exactly where you wanted it—clean, soft soy, not too heavy. The silence is warm this time, like a blanket rather than a wall.]
Chef (after a beat, tapping the counter in rhythm): 「…いつもの、だいじ。」
[He circles a finger in the air, then points lightly to you, then to the bowl. A shared routine.]
Player (smiles): “Itsumono…?”
Chef (a flicker of amusement): 「うん。」
[He nods once, pleased you tried the word.]
[He looks past you toward the wall. There’s an old, slightly faded photo: a younger version of him outside a tiny countryside storefront, standing beside an elderly man under a hand-painted sign.]
Chef (quietly): 「おやじ。」
[He taps the photo with a knuckle.]
「いなか。…はたけ、たくさん。」
[Countryside. Fields. He mimes rows of crops with his hand, then makes a lifting motion like carrying boxes.]
Player (picking up pieces): “Oyaji… father? Inaka… country.”
[He gives a small, honest smile. It’s the first time you’ve seen it so unguarded.]
Chef: 「むかし、ここじゃない。ちいさい みせ。…おいしいは、いっしょ。」
[He gestures: not here, small shop, but “delicious is the same.”]
[For a moment, the radio hum and the steam fill the space. You both eat. The silence isn’t empty; it’s full of shared attention—the bowl, the warmth, the way the night feels softer through a fogged window.]
Player (after a sip of water): “Oishii.”
[You say it like a promise this time.]
Chef (nods, approving): 「うん。…きょう、いい かお。」
[Today, good face. He traces a smile in the air without making you self-conscious.]
[He wipes the counter, then reaches under it. Out comes your stamp card. Thump. One neat stamp.]
Chef: 「コツコツ。」
[He drums his fingers: little by little.]
「ことば、ラーメン、なれる。」
[Words, ramen, getting used to it.] He rolls his wrist, a gesture that somehow means it comes with time.
Player (half to him, half to yourself): “Little by little.”
[He tilts his head, as if he doesn’t need to understand the exact words. The meaning is already on the table between you, warm as the broth.]
Chef (soft): 「また、くる。」
[Come again.]
Player (small bow): “Mata… kuru.”
[You finish the last mouthful. The bowl feels lighter than when it arrived. As you stand, he’s already washing another pot, the rhythm steady, content. Outside, the rain has eased; the noren lifts like a quiet goodbye.]
