Progression Summary
    •    1: First meeting; player notices her shelving books, she avoids eye contact and flees. Silent curiosity begins.
    •    2: She lingers while placing a book near the player; name revealed from her badge, mystery deepens.
    •    3: Hana whispers an inaudible greeting, panics, and runs away—interest is clear but her shyness overwhelms her.
    •    4: She approaches with an art book, freezes, retreats—her hidden passion begins to show.
    •    5: Shared silence at the same table; leaves a small note of gratitude behind. First real connection.
    •    6: Hana opens up—confesses loneliness, parental pressure to study law, secret love for art; shows her sketchbook.
    •    7: Resolution; she tells parents the truth, reconciliation follows. Farewell gift: a handmade art charm, symbol of trust and courage.



    Encounter 1 – First Meeting
    •    Player enters, confused about how things work. A friendly student (side character) explains:
    •    “Just pick any book and sit down, we read for an hour. Sometimes people share thoughts, but it’s casual.”
    •    The player notices a girl shelving books in the back. She glances at the player for a split second, then quickly avoids eye contact and rushes away.
    •    Player whispers to the student: “Who’s that?”
    •    Student shrugs: “Oh, Hana? She volunteers here. Doesn’t really… talk. Every time someone tries, she runs off. But she’s always around.”
    •    The scene ends with Hana briefly passing by your desk, silently placing a book down nearby before hurrying off.

Encounter 2 – Observation

Hana continues to orbit quietly.
    •    While you’re reading, Hana deliberately places a book close to you. This time, she lingers for a second before walking away.
    •    Camera pans to her badge: Hana.
    •    If you try to say “Thanks”, she bows quickly but doesn’t speak.
    •    The side character at the table whispers: “See? She’s like a ghost. Shows up, disappears. Honestly don’t think she has any friends.”

Encounter 3 – Tiny Spark

The first fragile attempt at interaction.
    •    Hana approaches your desk, holding a book. She opens her mouth like she wants to say something… but only whispers, almost inaudible.
    •    Player: “…What? Sorry, I didn’t catch that.”
    •    Hana flinches, bows quickly, and runs off.
    •    Student next to you chuckles softly: “Told you. She’s impossible to talk to.” 

Encounter 4 – Bold Attempt

Hana’s hesitation is visible now.
    •    She holds an art book in her arms, staring at you across the room.
    •    She walks halfway toward you, freezes, hugs the book tightly to her chest… then retreats back into the shelves.
    •    Player notices the title: “Impressionist Masters.”
    •    The player can choose whether to follow and pick up a similar art book, showing shared interest.

Encounter 5 – Shared Silence

Her walls begin to crack.
    •    Hana finally sits at the same table, pretending to read.
    •    She peeks over her book occasionally. If you look up, she hides again.
    •    At the end of the meetup, she leaves a small folded note on the desk but doesn’t hand it directly. Inside:
    •    “Thank you… for not giving up on me.”

Encounter 6 – Confession

Her conflict finally surfaces.
    •    Hana gathers courage and softly begins speaking in fragments. She reveals:
    •    She has no friends.
    •    She hates herself for being so shy, but can’t control it.
    •    She comes here to read art books in secret.
    •    Her parents are strict lawyers and expect her to follow the same path.
    •    She feels trapped—unable to say anything, unable to rebel.
    •    For the first time, she shows you her sketchbook. It’s filled with beautiful drawings, raw talent evident.
    •    Player can encourage her: “You should tell them how you feel.”

Encounter 7 – Farewell / Opening Up

The resolution of her arc.
    •    Hana meets you after the reading session. She’s trembling, but smiling.
    •    She explains softly:
    •    She finally told her parents the truth.
    •    They were angry—not because of her dream, but because she hid it so long.
    •    They apologized for not listening, and she apologized for not speaking.
    •    The conversation ended with mutual understanding. She will pursue art.
    •    Hana: “…You gave me courage. Without you, I… I’d still be hiding.”
    •    She hands you a small handmade charm — a tiny cloth strap with a brush-and-palette stitched in thread.
    •    “For you… so you don’t forget me. Like… you carried my dream, when I couldn’t.”
