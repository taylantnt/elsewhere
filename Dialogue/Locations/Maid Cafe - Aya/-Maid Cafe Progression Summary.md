
Progression Summary

    •    1: Awkward introduction, playful meal, curiosity.
    •    2: Recognition, banter, shares she studies English.
    •    3: Opens up about dreams, uncertain about singing.
    •    4: Talks about her study abroad & masks in society.
    •    5: Vulnerability about quitting café, family disapproval.
    •    6: New resolve, auditions/performance attempts.
    •    7: Farewell, real name reveal (Aya), gratitude, hope.

Encounter 1 — First Visit
    •    The player is surprised by the maid café concept.
    •    English-speaking maid greets them.
    •    First “Moe moe kyun” spell, some laughs, and the meal.
    •    She encourages the player to return.
    •    Tone: awkward but warm.

Encounter 2 — Recognition
    •    The maid recognizes the player: “Welcome back, Master! I remembered you!”
    •    She comments on how most foreigners only visit once out of curiosity, but he returned.
    •    They talk lightly about food choices; she teases him if he orders the same thing.
    •    Small personal reveal: She mentions she’s been studying English on her own, which is why she was chosen to help foreign customers.
    •    She asks the player:
    •    “What surprised you most about Japan so far?” (Choice-driven dialogue, but outcomes are flavor text).
    •    Tone: a little less formal, a little more playful. 

Encounter 3 — Opening Up
    •    She sits at the edge of the table for a short while (breaking café rules a bit).
    •    Talks about how working here is fun but can also be tiring, especially pretending to be cheerful all the time.
    •    Personal reveal: She actually likes performing, but not necessarily in this style. She once dreamed of being a singer or on stage somehow.
    •    She admits she isn’t sure if that dream is realistic.
    •    Player choice (encouraging / neutral):
    •    Encouraging her makes her blush slightly, saying “It’s easy for you to say, but… thank you. It helps hearing that.”
    •    Tone: the first glimpse of her real self.

    Encounter 4 — Cultural Exchange
    •    She asks the player more about their life:
    •    “Why did you come to Japan? Just to travel, or something more?”
    •    Depending on choice, she reacts differently but warmly.
    •    Personal reveal: She studied abroad once for a short time (maybe in Canada or Australia), and that’s why her English is so good. She loved the freedom there, but returning to Japan reminded her of the pressure to fit in.
    •    Philosophical moment: “Here, we wear uniforms, roles, even smiles like masks. Sometimes I wonder if people ever see the real me.”
    •    Tone: mutual curiosity, deepening trust.

Encounter 5 — Vulnerability
    •    She looks tired, maybe her voice a little hoarse, but she still greets warmly.
    •    She confesses she’s been thinking about quitting the café.
    •    Reason: her parents don’t really approve of this job—they think it’s frivolous, not respectable.
    •    Player can respond with sympathy or encouragement.
    •    If sympathetic: “You’re kind. Thank you for just listening.”
    •    If encouraging: “You make it sound so easy, but… I’m glad you believe in me.”
    •    This encounter ends with her saying: “I don’t know what I’ll do yet. But when I talk with you, I feel a little braver.”
    •    Tone: serious, emotional weight starts to show. 

Encounter 6 — Resolve
    •    The café feels quieter this time. She notices the player right away.
    •    She’s brighter again—something has changed.
    •    Personal reveal: She auditioned for something small—a singing contest, or performing at a local event. She was nervous, but just applying made her feel alive.
    •    She thanks the player for inspiring her courage, whether directly or indirectly.
    •    Dialogue has warmth: she’s not just a maid anymore, but a person sharing part of her journey.
    •    Player (thinking): realizes they’ve seen her grow bit by bit.
    •    Tone: hopeful, turning point.
    
Encounter 7 — Farewell / True Self
    •    She greets warmly but says this might be the last time they meet here. She might leave the café soon if her other path works out.
    •    She admits: “At first, I was just the ‘maid with pink hair.’ But with you, I could just… be myself. Not a character, not a mask.”
    •    Finally reveals her name: “My real name is Aya.”
    •    Soft emotional moment: she thanks the player sincerely for being part of her story.
    •    Tone: bittersweet but uplifting, showing growth and closure. 

    After she quits, player character can visit this store but without Aya present in the store and noone talks to our character like Aya did with the player character.
