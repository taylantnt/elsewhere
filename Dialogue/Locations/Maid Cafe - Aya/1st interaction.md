[Scene: Akihabara, Maid Café entrance]
Staff (Japanese): 「おかえりなさいませ、ご主人様！お一人様ですか？」(Welcome! Just one guest?)
Player (thinking): I nod, still unsure what I just walked into.
Staff (Japanese): 「少々お待ちください。英語対応できるスタッフを呼びますね。」(One moment please. I’ll call the staff who can assist in English.)
[A girl with pink hair, a fluffy white headband, and a short black-and-white maid dress steps forward, carrying a tray close to her chest. She smiles brightly.]
Maid: “Welcome, Master! Please, come this way.”
Player (thinking): …She’s cute. Really cute. I can’t tell if it’s her smile or the way her skirt sways when she walks, but I feel my heart jump a little.
[Player sits down at the table. The maid places a small laminated menu in front of them.]
Maid: “This is your first time here, yes? Don’t worry, I’ll explain how everything works. At our café, we don’t just serve food—we create a little magical experience. You are the Master, and we are here to make your day happy!”
Player: “I see… that sounds… unique.”
Maid (giggles): “You’ll get used to it! First, you can choose a set. They usually include a drink, food, and a small dessert. Or you can order individually. Would you like me to recommend something?”
[Choice appears:]
“Yes, please recommend something.”
“I’ll try choosing myself.”
“What do most people order?”
If player chooses 1:
Maid: “Then, may I suggest the omurice? It’s very fluffy, and… I get to draw something on it for you with ketchup. A little doodle, just for Master.”
Player (thinking): …She’s smiling so warmly, I can’t refuse.

If player chooses 2:
Player: “I’ll try picking something myself… Hmm.”
Maid (teasing): “Ooh, confident! Then I’ll watch closely to see what you choose.”
Player (thinking): Why does it feel like even ordering food is a test?

If player chooses 3:
Maid: “Most Masters try the omurice. It’s our specialty. But sweets are also very popular—like parfaits. Cute food for a cute café!”

[Regardless of choice, Player ends up ordering something.]
[Later, the maid brings the food to the table.]
Maid: “Here you are! Before you eat, we do a small magic spell together to make it extra delicious. Please, copy me.”
[She makes a heart shape with her hands and chants softly:]
Maid: “Moe moe… kyun!”
Player (hesitant): “…Moe moe… kyun.”
Maid (clapping): “Perfect! Now your food will be 100% more tasty.”
Player (thinking): I can’t believe I just did that. But… somehow, I’m smiling.

[Fade out as the Player eats. Time passes.]
Maid (later): “Did you enjoy your meal, Master?”
Player: “Yeah… more than I expected.”
Maid (smiling): “I’m glad! Please come visit again. Next time, maybe we can talk more. I’ll be waiting!”
Player (thinking): I don’t know if it’s the food or her energy, but… I think I really might come back.
[Scene fades.]
